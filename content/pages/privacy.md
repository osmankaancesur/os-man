# Privacy notice template

This demo can run locally. If you deploy it, replace this notice with one accurate for your site, hosting, database, and applicable law before accepting guestbook messages.

The guestbook stores a visitor's chosen name, note, submission time, and a random browser identifier. Names, notes, and timestamps are public. Reactions store the post, reaction, time, and browser identifier; only aggregate counts are public. A first party `visitor` cookie remembers the identifier for up to 365 days. Rate limit counters help prevent spam.

Theme, layout, keyboard, and audio preferences are kept in your browser's local storage. External Google Fonts requests can send network information to Google. This demo does not include analytics or advertising code.

To request removal of a note on a deployment, contact that deployment's operator. Add an operator contact and retention details here before deploying.
